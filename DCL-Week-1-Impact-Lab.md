# How Data Is Driving Better Decision-Making in Kaduna State, Nigeria

**DCL Data Analytics Fellowship | Week 1 Impact Lab**

## 1. Who Are They?

The Natview Foundation for Technology Innovation (NFTI) is a Nigerian non-profit organization that promotes the use of data, technology, and research to address social and development challenges. Its work focuses on supporting evidence-based decision-making in areas such as healthcare, education, and agriculture.

One of its notable initiatives is the Kaduna State Data Lab Project, which aimed to strengthen how public institutions collect, manage, and use data. According to NFTI's published case study, the initiative ran between 2018 and 2023, working with partners to improve the state's data infrastructure and support more informed public-sector decisions.

## 2. What Data Do They Use?

The Kaduna State Data Lab Project focused on strengthening health data systems and improving the availability, quality, and use of development-related statistics. NFTI's case study reports that the initiative connected 15 health data sources and supported the development of the Kaduna State Bureau of Statistics' capacity to produce and use reliable information.

The project addressed challenges such as inconsistent figures across data sources and limited access to detailed statistics at local government and ward levels. By improving coordination between data sources and strengthening statistical capacity, the initiative sought to make information more consistent, accessible, and useful for planning.

This matters because decision-makers need reliable data to identify gaps in public services, understand community needs, monitor progress, and make informed choices about resource allocation.

## 3. What Changed Because of It?

According to NFTI's case study, the initiative helped strengthen Kaduna State's data infrastructure, supported the connection of 15 health data sources, and trained 110 fellows. It also contributed to a shift towards making data platforms and statistical information more useful for government decision-making.

These improvements can help public institutions identify service gaps, compare needs across locations, monitor development priorities, and plan the use of public resources using stronger evidence.

The project demonstrates that creating impact is not simply about collecting data. It also requires reliable systems, skilled people, and a culture of using information to guide decisions. While these institutional improvements are important, they should not automatically be interpreted as proof that the project alone caused specific improvements in health outcomes.

## Conclusion

The Kaduna State Data Lab Project illustrates how data can support better governance when it is reliable, accessible, and actively used. NFTI's work highlights the importance of investing in data infrastructure and analytical capacity so that public institutions can better understand the needs of the communities they serve.

As a data analytics fellow, my key takeaway is that the real value of data lies not only in collecting and analyzing it, but in using the resulting insights to make better decisions and improve people's lives.
